# Hack ideas

← [Docs index](README.md)

Pick one of these product ideas as the story for your demo, or remix them. Each builds on one
or more tracks plus the [assistant](assistant-and-finale.md).

### 1. Disruption Command Center
Explains the impact of a disruption in plain language and sends rebooking priorities to
`automation_queue` for a robot or agent to act on.
**Builds on:** [Disruptions](track-3-disruptions.md), [Flight plans](track-2-flight-plans.md), [UiPath hand-off](uipath-handoff.md).

### 2. Passenger Flow & Security Queue Predictor
Forecasts queue peaks per terminal and checkpoint and gives staffing advice (how many lanes,
and when) before the peak hits.
**Builds on:** [Passenger onboarding](track-1-passenger-onboarding.md), [Flight plans](track-2-flight-plans.md).

### 3. Gate & Stand Allocation Optimiser
Detects stand conflicts and proposes reassignments, each with a short explanation a planner
can accept or reject.
**Builds on:** [Gates & baggage](track-4-gates-and-baggage.md), [Flight plans](track-2-flight-plans.md).

### 4. Transfer Baggage Risk Tracker
Flags bags on short or broken connections and drafts task lists for handlers, grouped by
outbound flight and gate.
**Builds on:** [Gates & baggage](track-4-gates-and-baggage.md), [Disruptions](track-3-disruptions.md).

### 5. Route Network & Sustainability Explorer
A connectivity map of CPH with "what if" questions (e.g. "what if we lose the Frankfurt
route?") and estimated CO₂ per route from great-circle distance.
**Builds on:** [Flight plans](track-2-flight-plans.md), [Disruptions](track-3-disruptions.md).
